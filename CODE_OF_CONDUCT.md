# Code of conduct

This applies to the workshop itself and to this repository's community spaces
(issues, pull requests and discussions).

## Our standard

We are a community of researchers practising critical, respectful enquiry. We expect
everyone to treat others with courtesy and good faith, especially in disagreement, and
to welcome scepticism and dissent about tools and ideas, since challenging a claim is
encouraged and demeaning a person is not. We expect consideration for differing
levels of experience, so that no question about a tool is too basic, and respect for
privacy and confidentiality, so that no one shares others' data, words or work without
consent, online or in the room.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, demeaning comments, sustained disruption
and sharing others' personal or confidential information without consent.

## Scope

The workshop room, the breakout groups, the shared notes and any repository space
connected to this project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the facilitator at
pablo.bernabeu@education.ox.ac.uk. Reports will be handled discreetly and in line with
institutional policy. During the session, you may raise a concern with the facilitator
directly and in confidence.

This is a lightweight, workshop-specific code. It does not replace your institution's
own policy, which continues to apply.

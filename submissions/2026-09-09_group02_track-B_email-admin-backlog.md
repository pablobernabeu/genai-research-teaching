*Scenario: B · Accessible Executive-Function Layer*

**The problem.** Tame an email or admin backlog

**The artefact.** ChatGPT

> Email Subject: Checking In
>
> Dear [Student’s Name],
>
> I just wanted to check in as I have noticed that you haven’t been in class for the past four weeks. More importantly, I wanted to make sure that you are okay.
>
> I understand that personal circumstances can sometimes make it difficult to attend or keep up with university work. You don’t need to share anything you are uncomfortable discussing, but please do get in touch so that I know how you are and we can look at any support you may need.
>
> As this is your final year, regular attendance is important and will help you stay on track with your learning, assessments and graduation requirements. The University also monitors attendance and engagement, so I would really like us to address this before you fall further behind.
>
> Please reply within the next two working days, even if it is just to let me know that you are okay. We can then arrange a brief chat and work out the best way forward together.
>
> Warm regards,
>
> [Your Name]\
> Module Leader\
> [Module Name and Code]

**Errors caught.**

- Tone of voice around compassion and formality
- Specifying exact time to respond
- Stating the University policy mildly

**Automation–steering map.** We steered by overriding with our human voice

**Oversight model.** Interwoven (checking throughout)

**Why that model.** For the task type, it seemed like the only reasonable approach

**Key insight.** Emphasis on information versus tone and human connection
